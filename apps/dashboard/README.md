# Vocaweb Dashboard

**Build websites just by talking or typing.** Vocaweb is an AI-powered website builder — you describe what you want, and Vocaweb builds it live in front of you. No coding needed.

---

## How It Works

Vocaweb works in 3 simple steps:

1. **Connect your wallet** — Sign in with your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) or just your email
2. **Describe your website** — Use voice or chat to tell Vocaweb what you want
3. **Watch it build live** — Your website appears in real time. Ask for changes, and Vocaweb updates it instantly

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign In

When you open the app, click **Connect** on the welcome screen. You can sign in with:

- **Solana Wallet** — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any detected wallet
- **Email** — Get a login code sent to your inbox
- **SMS** — Sign in with your phone number

Don't have a wallet? No problem — Vocaweb automatically creates an embedded wallet for you.

After connecting, pick a username and you're in.

### Step 2: Choose Your Model

Vocaweb has 3 AI models. Click the model selector in the chat box to switch:

| Model | Access |
|-------|--------|
| **Vocaweb v1 (Base)** | Free — 2 trial builds for everyone |
| **Vocaweb v2 (Pro)** | Hold 250,000 $DROOP tokens |
| **Vocaweb v3 (Max)** | Hold 1,000,000 $DROOP tokens |

Your wallet is checked automatically — if you hold enough $DROOP, the model unlocks instantly.

### Step 3: Build Your Website

You have two ways to build:

#### Chat Mode (Type it)

1. Click the **Chat** toggle at the top
2. Type what you want: *"Build me a portfolio website with a dark theme and a photo gallery"*
3. Attach reference images if you want (optional)
4. Hit send — Vocaweb starts building immediately
5. Keep chatting to make changes: *"Make the header bigger"*, *"Add a contact form"*

#### Voice Mode (Say it)

1. Click the **Voice** toggle at the top
2. Press the mic button and start talking
3. Vocaweb listens, responds, and builds while you speak
4. It's a real conversation — interrupt, clarify, or change direction anytime

## The Sandbox — Your Live Preview

Once your website starts building, the **Sandbox** unlocks in the sidebar. This is where you see everything:

- **Live Preview** — Your actual website running in the browser, updating in real time
- **File Explorer** — Browse every file Vocaweb created
- **Code Viewer** — See the code with syntax highlighting
- **Click to Edit** — Click any element in the preview and tell Vocaweb to change exactly that part

---

## Import From Your Favorite Tools

Vocaweb connects to the tools you already use. Go to **Settings → Integrations** to connect:

- **Figma** — Import your designs and Vocaweb turns them into real websites
- **Notion** — Pull content from your Notion pages straight into your site
- **Canva** — Bring your Canva designs into your projects

Once connected, use the **Import** button in chat to bring your designs in.

---

## Chat History

Every conversation is saved. Click **Chat History** in the sidebar to:

- See all your past chat and voice sessions
- Resume any session right where you left off
- Reopen the project files from that session
- Delete sessions you don't need

---

## Publish Your Website

When your website is ready:

1. Click **Publish** in the sandbox
2. Vocaweb deploys it to a live URL instantly
3. Want a custom domain? Add it in the publish settings

Your website is now live on the internet.

---

## Settings

Click **Settings** in the sidebar to manage:

- **Account** — Your email, wallet, and username
- **Integrations** — Connect or disconnect Figma, Notion, Canva
- **Appearance** — Switch between light and dark mode

## FAQ

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Vocaweb writes all the code for you. You just describe what you want.

**Is voice mode really real-time?**
Yes. Vocaweb listens and responds as you speak, like a phone call with a designer.

**What happens to my projects?**
They're saved to your account and synced across devices. Resume anytime from Chat History.

**How do I unlock v2 and v3 models?**
Hold $DROOP tokens in your connected wallet. 250K for v2, 1M for v3. Verification is automatic.

**Can I edit specific parts of my site?**
Yes — open the Sandbox, click any element in the preview, and tell Vocaweb what to change.
