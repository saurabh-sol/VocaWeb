# Startup Branding

**Purpose:** Establish a lightweight but coherent brand identity for a new product/startup.

**When to use:** When there's no existing brand and one needs to be defined from scratch.

## Core Principles

- Define in order: name/tagline -> one primary brand color -> logo mark/wordmark -> type pairing -> tone of voice.
- Keep the initial brand system minimal — a startup needs 1 primary color + neutrals + 1-2 semantic colors, not a full palette.
- Tone of voice should be defined in a sentence or two (e.g. 'confident but approachable') and applied consistently in copy.
- Logo should work at small sizes (favicon) and in a single color before anything more elaborate is attempted.
- Keep brand decisions easy to evolve — avoid over-investing in visual identity before product-market fit.

## Checklist

- [ ] Minimal, coherent color/type system defined
- [ ] Tone of voice documented and applied in copy
- [ ] Logo legible at favicon size and in single color

## Common Pitfalls

- Over-engineering a full brand system before validating the product
- Logo that breaks down at small sizes
